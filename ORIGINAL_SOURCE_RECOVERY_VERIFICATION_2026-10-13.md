## Interpretation constraints

Exact four-state closure is calculated before ensemble aggregation. Medians of separately aggregated climate, LULC and interaction effects do not necessarily sum to the median of their combined response, so that difference is not evidence of failed per-cell closure.

Zero original Python script or Jupyter notebook files were present in the recovered ZIP. The original full Direct-Hou cellwise predicted probabilities and exact original 2061–2080 interpolation–emulation and spatial S19 scripts were not recovered. These cannot be claimed as reproduced by the existing compact public GitHub workflow.

The private author-original folder is not cleared for public redistribution. The manuscript-reported Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22713203 is not independently verified.
