DATA AVAILABILITY STATEMENT (paste into the manuscript's Data Availability section)

The historical feature table, out-of-fold predictions, factorial decomposition
outputs (harmonized, reconstructed, and Direct-Hou), threshold-sensitivity
results, applicability-domain diagnostics, Core-7 dependence weights, the
predictor correlation matrix, and all code needed to reproduce the paper's
reported numerical results are available at
https://github.com/Quist-ish/volta-flood-climate-lulc-scenario-interface
and archived with a permanent identifier at Zenodo
(DOI: [insert upon deposit — see ZENODO_UPLOAD_CHECKLIST.md]).

Third-party source datasets (CHIRPS, ERA5-Land, NEX-GDDP-CMIP6, the Global
Flood Database, JRC Global Surface Water, MERIT Hydro, MODIS MCD12Q1, the
Hou et al. (2022) future land-use/land-cover projections, WorldPop,
OpenStreetMap, and the GRDC Major River Basins boundary product) are not
redistributed in the repository. Exact product versions, access dates, and
provider citations are listed in the repository's manifests
(manifests/external_data_sources.csv); these datasets should be obtained
directly from their original providers under their respective licenses
(see DATA_LICENSE_NOTICE.md).

The repository distinguishes, without fabrication, which components of the
analysis were independently re-executed and verified from the deposited
archive and which require the corresponding author's local project
environment to regenerate from raw provider data (see RELEASE_STATUS.md).
